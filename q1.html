Separating content from presentation

Explanation

The "em" and "strong" tags are better than the "i" and "b" tags. I say this because "em" and "strong" are more visible in coding spaces. They provide a more structured look to the environment. Another plus I learned recently is that "em" and "strong" tags are compatible with most screen readers. This proves useful when creating a fully accessible environment. However, "i" and "b" tags are useful when you want to change text styling quickly.
